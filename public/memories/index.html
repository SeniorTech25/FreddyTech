Before you hand over a tape

What if the label is missing?

Choose “Not sure” for the quantity or leave the formats blank. We can start with the shape of the cassette.

What if a tape looks damaged?

Leave it in its case and describe what you see. Playback and recovery depend on the condition and equipment.

Who gets access, and what happens to my originals?

Before arranging a handoff, ask Freddy to confirm who will handle the recordings, how you’ll receive the files, when copies will be deleted and how the tapes will come back to you.
